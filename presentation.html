Technical Depth

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

1. Ingestion

In-memory byte extraction via pypdf and multi-encoding text decoding without writing temp files to disk.

2. Chunking

Sliding window chunking with 400-word windows and 80-word overlap to preserve context across boundaries.

3. Vector Search

TfidfVectorizer with n-grams (1,2) and sublinear scaling, coupled with Cosine Similarity ranking.

Direct Full-Text Fallback

For documents under 35,000 characters, the full text is provided directly to Gemini's 1M-token context window, allowing seamless document summaries and high-level syntheses.
